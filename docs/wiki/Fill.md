# Fill

**id** `fill` · **key** `G`

Flood fill modes: `color`, `pattern` (from [[Libraries]]), `maze`, `puzzle`.

Maze paints walls in the primary color and corridors in the secondary; puzzle paints outlines in the primary and pieces in the secondary — see [[Tools#Colors]].
